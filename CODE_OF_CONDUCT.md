# Code of Conduct

Zo Media Intelligence is an open-source engineering project. Participation is welcome from people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas, code, evidence, and tradeoffs rather than attacking people.
- Give contributors enough context to reproduce a problem.
- Respect privacy: never post real recordings, transcripts, credentials, customer names, private paths, or other sensitive data.
- Accept technical review in good faith and keep disagreements focused on the work.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or deliberately disrupt other participants.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when behavior undermines a safe, productive technical community. Enforcement decisions should be proportional and documented when practical.

## Reporting

For ordinary conduct concerns, contact the maintainer privately through the contact information on the GitHub profile. For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of opening a public issue.

By participating in this project you agree to follow this Code of Conduct.
